IMPORTANT NOTICE

All GluteBase Members

This is a strict warning to all members. Please read carefully.

GluteBase equipment is expensive and must be handled responsibly.

1. Dumbbells & Weights

Do not throw, slam or carelessly drop weights. Always return them to their designated places.

2. Machines

Control the weights while lifting and lowering. Do not bang or misuse equipment.

3. Gym Property

Damaging benches, breaking equipment, misplacing or stealing even small accessories will NOT be tolerated.

Strict Action Will Be Taken:
  • • Equipment damage and missing items are monitored and recorded.
  • • Anyone responsible will have to bear the repair or replacement cost.
  • • Serious or repeated violations will result in immediate membership cancellation without a refund.

We have 300+ members. One person’s carelessness should not affect everyone else’s safety and experience.

Lift heavy, but handle equipment responsibly.
Respect the space, the equipment and fellow members.
We will not tolerate damage, theft or indiscipline.
— Team GluteBase
