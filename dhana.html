About Fitwolf

With nearly a decade of experience in the fitness industry, Dhana, widely known as Fitwolf, brings hands-on coaching experience, a client-centric approach and a strong understanding of sustainable fitness.

Having worked with multiple gyms across Bangalore and trained individuals across different age groups and fitness levels, he founded Glute Base with a clear vision: to move beyond conventional gym training and create a more personalised, inclusive and supportive fitness environment.

His training philosophy is built on three fundamentals: discipline, consistency and individualised guidance. His approach prioritises long-term physical well-being, strength, confidence and sustainable lifestyle changes over quick fixes and temporary transformations.

At Glute Base, Dhana leads with a belief that fitness should be accessible, adaptable and built around the individual. His focus is not simply to transform bodies, but to help people develop habits, strength and confidence that last.
